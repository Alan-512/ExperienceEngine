## Why

ExperienceEngine governs guidance after harm, but the matched-block evidence layer has no causal sequence proving that an actually delivered harmful intervention produces authoritative harm evidence and suppresses the same guidance on a later equivalent opportunity.

## What Changes

- Define a sealed harm-exposure and recovery-recheck sequence.
- Require authoritative feedback/attribution and production-governed delivery-state transition evidence.
- Reject direct benchmark writes to harmed counters or lifecycle/delivery state.
- Add supplemental harm-recovery coverage and success metrics.
- Add deterministic fixtures before real-host execution.

## Capabilities

- `matched-block-benchmark-evidence`: Production-path harm-recovery sequence evidence.

## Dependencies

- `add-benchmark-decision-opportunity-evidence`
